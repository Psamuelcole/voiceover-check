Most small businesses do not fail because of a bad product. They fail because nobody knows they exist.
In 2019, one bakery spent two hundred dollars on flyers and got thirty nine new customers.
The next year, the owner stopped paying for ads and asked every customer for one referral.
Sales grew by sixty five percent, and the marketing bill fell to zero.
The lesson is simple: your best customers are not expensive to find. They already know you.
